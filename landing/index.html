HOW IT WORKS

1

Download & Install

Download the installer for your platform (Windows .exe or macOS .dmg) and run it. The app installs like any other desktop application.

2

Enter Your License Key

On first launch, enter your Aurum license key (format: AURUM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) along with your username and password.

3

Configure Broker & Mode

In Settings, select your broker (Topstep, Tradovate, or Interactive Brokers), paste your API key (stored encrypted), and choose Evaluation or Funded account mode.

4

Start the Bot

Click START BOT in the Control Center. The bot connects to your broker, subscribes to market data, and begins scanning for MNQ NY Open setups from 09:30–10:30 ET.

5

Bot Runs While App Is Open

Trading only occurs while the desktop app is open and your computer is running. Close the app and the bot stops immediately — no background execution.
